Few Words About Us

Founded by culinary visionary Kugendran, our family of nine distinct restaurants—Thairoom, ThairoomGrand, Savor, Lelert, Red Onion, Aamara, Kao Kang, and Same Same But Different Thai—offers a dynamic and diverse dining experience rooted in passion for flavor, culture, and creativity.

Each restaurant brings its own unique personality to the table, while celebrating the rich traditions of Thai, Indian, and Continental cuisine. From modern interpretations of classic street food to elegant, elevated dining, our venues serve up authentic taste with a contemporary twist.
