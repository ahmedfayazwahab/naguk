Few Words About Us

Founded by culinary visionary Kugendran, Naguk Inc. is home to eleven distinct restaurant brands across the Greater Toronto Area — thairoom, thairoomgrand, Savor, Kohlipe, Kao Kang, Le Lert, Somtum Jinda, Red Onion, Gabbys, and Mumbaiya Misal Pav.

Each brand brings its own personality to the table, celebrating the rich traditions of Thai, Indian, and Continental cuisine — from Michelin-recognized fine dining to beloved neighbourhood kitchens. From modern interpretations of classic street food to elegant elevated experiences, every Naguk restaurant is rooted in passion for flavour, culture, and creativity.
